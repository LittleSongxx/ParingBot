# node1 rocketmq-proxy OOM 证据（2026-10-18）

docker inspect rocketmq-proxy:
  restarts=10
  memory=536870912 (512 MiB before fix)
  state.OOMKilled=false (Docker 的当前状态标志，不代表历史)
  started=2026-10-17T15:34:36Z

dmesg:
  2026-10-17 23:33:50 node1 Memory cgroup out of memory:
  Killed process 687503 (java), memcg docker-bc1123ad0672..., task=java, anon-rss=490704kB
  （该 memcg 对应 rocketmq-proxy）

docker logs rocketmq-proxy:
  多次出现 "Killed" → "startup successfully"，重启计数 10

处置：
  docker update --memory 805306368 --memory-swap 805306368 rocketmq-proxy
  运行态 memory=805306368，proxy gRPC 端口可用
  deploy/compose.yaml: mem_limit 384m→768m，JAVA_OPT_EXT Xmx256m→384m
